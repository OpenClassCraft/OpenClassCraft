Questions schools ask

Before you bring it into a lab.

Has OpenClassCraft officially released?

The source and Community preview are public. A tagged download is only an official alpha when it appears on the project’s Releases page and passes the release checklist. The School Console is a controlled beta, not a finished commercial product.

Why not charge every student?

Per-student accounts add cost and administration before learning begins. OpenClassCraft keeps the core game free and uses one predictable campus price for implementation support.

Does it need the internet or student accounts?

The current classroom flow is local: one machine hosts on a trusted LAN and the Teacher Console stores its workspace locally. Internet access may still be needed to download software and updates.

What student data should a pilot collect?

Use classroom aliases or group names only. Do not enter dates of birth, personal email addresses, health information, government identifiers, or other sensitive student records.

Can a government school use OpenClassCraft?

Yes. Government schools in any region may apply for sponsored School Edition access at no charge. Installation on government-managed systems must still follow the applicable education-department, security, privacy, and software-installation procedures; OpenClassCraft cannot grant those local approvals.
