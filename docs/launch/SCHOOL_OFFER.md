# Founding School Beta offer

Status: owner-review commercial proposal, 26 August 2026. This document is not an invoice, licence, tax opinion, or binding contract. Confirm commercial terms in a written agreement before accepting payment.

## Positioning

> OpenClassCraft is an offline, open-source voxel coding classroom for Grades 5–8. Students build programs as physical blocks, run them through a robot, and debug the result together on the school LAN.

The Community game remains free. The paid offer makes adoption dependable for a school through onboarding, operational tooling, teacher training, starter curriculum, and support.

## Guided beta scope

The ₹4,999 guided beta includes:

- one campus and one teacher champion;
- an initial lab/network readiness call;
- controlled access to the School Console beta;
- the Community game and one starter coding lesson;
- one remote teacher workshop;
- a pilot plan for up to four classroom sessions;
- weekly remote support within an agreed response window; and
- an end-of-pilot review.

If the school signs a founding annual plan within 30 days of a successful pilot, credit the ₹4,999 pilot fee against the first-year price. State this credit in the written pilot agreement.

## Founding annual scope

The proposed ₹12,499 first-year campus plan includes:

- all students at one physical campus;
- up to ten named teacher users of the operational material;
- School Console builds and eligible updates during the term;
- two remote teacher-training sessions;
- starter lesson/curriculum packs delivered during the term;
- documented email or scheduled-call support within the agreed boundary; and
- continued access to school-created local worlds and exports after the term.

It does not include custom game development, onsite travel, device/network repair, guaranteed compatibility with unmanaged hardware, student data migration, cloud hosting, curriculum accreditation, or government approval. Quote these separately only when there is capacity and a written scope.

## Qualification rules

A founding pilot requires:

- a decision-maker who approves beta use;
- one teacher champion who can attend onboarding;
- a suitable computer lab and trusted LAN;
- permission to run at least three 45-minute sessions;
- agreement to use aliases and the pilot data-minimisation rules;
- weekly feedback; and
- acceptance that beta defects may require rescheduling.

Free private-school pilots are selected for fit and evidence value, not merely arrival order. Prefer variation in school size and device setup while keeping travel practical.

## Government-school sponsorship

Eligible, properly authorised government schools in any region may apply for a sponsored School Edition licence at no charge. Sponsorship covers the agreed licence term and does not bypass the school, education department, privacy, security, network, procurement, or software-installation requirements that apply in that region. It is an OpenClassCraft programme, not an endorsement, partnership, certification, or procurement commitment by any government or education authority.

Do not install or demonstrate on managed school systems until the responsible local authority approves the exact package and procedure. Record the approval scope and licence term in the written pilot agreement.
